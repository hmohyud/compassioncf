Board members

The people behind the work

Meet the board guiding the Compassion Course Foundation.

Cofounder and Executive Director

Thom Bond

Thom Bond is best known as the creator and leader of The Compassion Course, a comprehensive online training that has served over 50,000 participants and been translated into 20 languages in over 120 countries.

Thom is the author of The Compassion Book, the creator of “The Exercise – Shifting Toward Compassion” (theexercise.org), and “64 Days for Peace”, an open source, online, self-led small-group introductory curriculum.

He is a founder and the Director of Education for NYCNVC (The New York Center for Nonviolent Communication, a United Nations Civil Society Organization) and the founder and Director of Education for Bushfield Farms (a demonstration farm and educational center, focused on sustainability and social reform). He is a former member and Advisory Board Member for the Communications Coordination Committee for the United Nations (CCCUN).

Secretary and Treasurer

Debra Chew, M.D., M.P.H

Dr. Debra Chew, M.D., MPH, is a physician, epidemiologist, educator, and public health leader committed to advancing compassionate, evidence-based care. She has more than three decades of experience in patient care, medical education, hospital leadership, and scientific research, in academia and public health, most recently as the Associate Professor of Medicine at Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and University Hospital in Newark, New Jersey.

Dr. Chew has trained in Nonviolent Communication and Needs Based Awareness, as a participant of The Compassion Course. She will help lead the Global Peace Education Research Initiative.

Chief Information Officer

Hyder Mohyuddin

Hyder Mohyuddin is a software engineer who built this website and maintains the Compassion Course’s online platform.

Hyder has a major in Computer Science and a minor in Visual Arts from the University of Chicago, and worked there on generative image research. A tutor since 2016, Hyder has also helped teach coding with Code Platoon and Code Your Dreams.
