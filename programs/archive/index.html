Becoming an online library of audio courses

Creating a multi-lingual, global library by digitizing, translating, and indexing 25 years of archival materials from The Compassion Course conferences, workshops, and teachings from pioneers in nonviolent communication

Voices in the collection

  • Thom BondFounder of The Compassion Course, Co-Founder of the New York Center for Nonviolent Communication (NYCNVC), and Author of The Compassion Book
  • Robert GonzalesFounder of The Center for Living Compassion and Pioneer in Spiritual Psychology
  • Marshall RosenbergFounder of Nonviolent Communication and Author of Nonviolent Communication: A Language of Life

From recordings to a living library

The Archive Program is tasked with acquiring, preserving, translating, organizing and distributing the teachings in the form of an online library of audio courses in over 240 languages.

  1. AcquireCompleteGathering decades of conference and workshop recordings.
  2. ProtectProtecting the original recordings for posterity.
  3. TranslateTranslating the teachings throughout the globe in over 240 languages.
  4. OrganizeOrganizing a searchable resource, carefully curated for reference and self-study.
  5. DistributeDistributing our digital library around the world.
