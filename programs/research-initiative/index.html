The Global Peace Education Research Initiative

Peace Education Research On A Global Scale

Using validated psychological instruments and real human stories to measure how compassion training transforms conflict behavior across borders

Why Research Matters

To scale peace education worldwide, educators, policymakers, and funders need scientific evidence that peace education training creates lasting behavioral change for compassion and conflict-resolution. We will analyze behavioral shifts across thousands of learners from diverse backgrounds and regions within our global Compassion Course community. Through robust qualitative and quantitative science, we aim to capture the impact of our training and behavioral shifts in nonviolent communication, self-compassion and empathy.
