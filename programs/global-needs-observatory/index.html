Global Human Needs Observatory

A needs-based framework and infrastructure for peace engineering

Mapping universal human needs, procuring resource data, and applying Intelligence to create breakthrough conflict resolution strategies.

What the Observatory is

Rooted in the Needs Awareness framework, the Global Human Needs Observatory is a scientifically credible system to identify and track universal human needs (such as autonomy, connection, meaning, safety, nutrition, security) on an individual, group, community and regional level, intelligently linking lived experiences to verified local resources.
