The Peace Engineering Scholarship Fund

Ending Financial Gatekeeping in Peace Education

Scholarships and logistical support, so that peace education is never limited by privilege or circumstance.

To ensure that peace education is available to all, the Fund provides scholarships and logistical support to individuals and groups who would otherwise face financial or geographic barriers

What the Fund does

  • 01Expands global access to high-quality peace education
  • 02Supports underserved communities, including those affected by conflict, displacement, or economic hardship
  • 03Accelerates the spread of compassionate communication tools
  • 04Strengthens networks of peace-builders who bring these skills back to their families, organizations, and communities
The Fund ensures that the benefits of peace education are not limited by privilege or circumstance.

Give Someone the Opportunity to Learn

A gift of $240 provides one person with a full-year scholarship to the Compassion Course, and helps remove financial barriers to peace education.

All contributions are administered by the Compassion Course Foundation for its scholarship program. The Foundation retains discretion over the selection of recipients and distribution of awards in accordance with its scholarship policies.
