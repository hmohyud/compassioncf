What Changes

When people learn peace, people change.

Communities are facing rising levels of polarization, stress, and conflict. Research across psychology, education, and peace studies consistently shows that peace is most effectively built by cultivating the skills, mindsets, and relationships that prevent conflict in the first place.

Studies from UNESCO, the Global Partnership for the Prevention of Armed Conflict, and numerous academic programs have affirmed that peace education contributes to:

  1. 01Reduced interpersonal and community violence
  2. 02Improved emotional resilience and self-regulation
  3. 03Greater social cohesion and empathy across cultures
  4. 04Increased capacity for collaboration and conflict transformation
“When people learn concrete tools for connection and compassion, they create environments where everyone’s needs matter.”

Our Vision: a world where compassion guides our interactions and creates positive change in communities everywhere.
