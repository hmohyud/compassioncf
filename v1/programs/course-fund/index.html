Removing Barriers

The Compassion Course Fund

Scholarships and logistical support, so that peace education is never limited by privilege or circumstance.

To ensure that peace education is available to all, the Compassion Course Fund provides scholarships and logistical support to individuals and groups who would otherwise face financial or geographic barriers.

What the Fund does

By doing so, the Fund:

  • 01Expands global access to high-quality peace education
  • 02Supports underserved communities, including those affected by conflict, displacement, or economic hardship
  • 03Accelerates the spread of compassionate communication tools
  • 04Strengthens networks of peace-builders who bring these skills back to their families, organizations, and communities
The Fund ensures that the benefits of the Compassion Course are not limited by privilege or circumstance.

It fuels a movement that empowers people everywhere to contribute to a more peaceful world.
