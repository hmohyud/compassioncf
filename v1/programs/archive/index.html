Becoming an online library of audio courses

The CCF has acquired hundreds of hours of digital content, reaching back almost 25 years, of Compassion Course conferences and workshops.

Voices in the collection

  • Thom BondCreator of The Compassion Course
  • Robert GonzalesConference and workshop content
  • Marshall RosenbergNever-before-seen 720p video

From recordings to a living library

The Archive Program is tasked with acquiring, protecting, translating, organizing and distributing this knowledge in the form of an online library of audio courses in 249 languages.

  1. AcquireCompleteGathering decades of conference and workshop recordings.
  2. ProtectPreserving the original recordings for the long term.
  3. TranslateCarrying the teaching into 249 languages.
  4. OrganizeShaping the material into audio courses.
  5. DistributeOpening it to the world as an online library.

The first phase of content acquisition is complete and will shift to maintenance mode after the virtual library opens. The target date for opening is June 24, 2026.
